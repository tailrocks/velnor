## Seat Tiers

Your seat tier sets how many people inside your organization may access and work with the Licensed Materials. Choose the tier that matches the number of developers who will actually use the files, and upgrade if your team grows beyond it.

### Personal

For a single developer, freelancer, or solo creator. This tier covers one named user, and only that person may view, edit, or build with the Licensed Materials.

### Team

For small teams. This tier covers up to 5 people who belong to the same company or organization.

### Growth

For growing teams that need more room and a few procurement perks. This tier covers up to 10 people in the same company or organization, and adds priority support along with wire-transfer payment and custom invoicing against a purchase order.

### Enterprise

For larger organizations and agencies. This tier covers an unlimited number of people across your organization and includes priority support.

## Client Work & Handover

Building for clients is covered by your license, and you may hand the finished product over to them. What your license covers is **you and the people on your seats**. It does not extend to the client, to their in-house developers, or to another agency that picks the project up later.

The line is whether the recipient works with the Licensed Materials themselves. Delivering an end product that a client simply runs, deploys, or asks you to maintain requires nothing further from them. If the client or any third party will **modify, extend, or continue building with the ReUI source** \- editing the delivered components, installing further items from the registry, or reusing them in other projects - that is use of the Licensed Materials by someone your license does not cover, and they need their own license, purchased in their own name.

This works the same way in both directions: a license cannot be transferred to a client as part of a handover, and a client cannot keep working from your key once the engagement ends. Seat tiers are listed on our [pricing page](https://reui.io/pricing), and if you are not sure which side of the line an arrangement falls on, [email us](mailto:hello@reui.io) before you deliver.
